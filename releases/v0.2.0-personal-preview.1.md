## Changelog
- Add a native DualSense emulation toggle, with compatibility controller selection when disabled.
- Add Automatic / Always on / Off modes for native controller audio and HD haptics, including in-stream Quick Menu controls.
- Automatic mode waits for 20 stable seconds at a configured video bitrate of at least 12 Mbps, pauses on poor connectivity or RTT of at least 150 ms, and resumes below 120 ms after stabilization.
- Negotiate native PCM traffic over the existing encrypted control connection, renewing a short host lease so traffic can stop at its source.
- Keep controller identity stable during connection-quality adaptation; ordinary inputs, rumble, adaptive triggers and LEDs continue independently of the PCM mode.
- Handle revoked microphone permission; correct startup test setup.
- Add personal testing identity, disable inherited automatic updates, and add build notices.

## Validation and known limits
- ARM64 APK compilation and native feedback policy checks passed in the linked CI run.
- Earlier local verification passed targeted controller/startup tests and APK signature checks.
- The inherited Android lint baseline is not clean; lint diagnostics are not a release certification.
- Direct Bluetooth support depends on Android version, permissions and device support; USB and optional bridge paths inherit upstream requirements.
- End-to-end physical DualSense haptics, controller audio, adaptive triggers and automatic network adaptation remain hardware-unverified.
- Use the matching personal Artemis and Apollo previews for the new traffic-policy negotiation. Changing controller emulation can require reconnecting and restarting the game.
